/ 03 — AI film

Hanuman: Dhyana.

A 33-second devotional film, made with AI from the first frame to the last.

Hanuman meditates on a forest peak at sunrise. The film never shows him all at once: it reveals him piece by piece, the tail, the hands, the halo, and keeps him in silhouette until his eyes open.

  1. Story and shot planA 30-second story in 12 shots, with a colour script and music hit points.
  2. Character sheetHis look is locked first, so every shot matches.
  3. Storyboard framesA cinematic still for each shot, picked from two takes.
  4. AI videoImage to video with Kling 3.0 at 1080p, shot by shot.
  5. Edit and titleCut to the music, finished with an animated gold title.
