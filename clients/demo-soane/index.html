/ Services

Four ways to work with us.

We take three private residences a year. Beyond that, we consult on small hospitality and one heritage restoration project per year.

— 01

Full residence

Concept, layout, interior architecture, FFE — through to opening day. 9—14 month build.

— 02

Hospitality

Cafes, boutique stays and small restaurants. We co-design with your brand & ops.

— 03

Heritage restoration

Pre-1970 homes only. We work with the original drawings where they survive — and the building, where they don't.

— 04

Consult / advisory

For owners working with another architect who'd like an independent eye on the interiors brief.
